# V0.3 feature specification

## Conveyor and shredder

The central belt transports server-owned junk from z=-60 to z=66 at 3 studs/second. Full trip: 42 seconds. A new weighted machine spawns every 7 seconds; four spaced machines seed startup. Normal belt spacing is 21 studs, with short-range 48-stud nameplates to reduce overlap. Spawn weights and all 18 machine models remain in MachineConfig. No machine is granted to a new profile.

The server updates anchored models every 0.1 seconds. At the deadline it removes pickup eligibility and destroys the model; clients play a brief shred flash. Pickup checks expiry independently, so a delayed movement tick cannot rescue already-expired junk. The shredder has physical walls, a dark mouth and locally animated rotors. Salvage cannot be duplicated by two simultaneous pickup requests: ownership is removed before a carry is created.

DROP releases carried junk into the world for 25 seconds. Death/disconnect also drop held junk. Ground drops remain still and can be reclaimed; they expire without a shredder flash. The spawn cap is 12 world items; drops are allowed beyond that cap so they are not silently lost. Stored yard inventory is unaffected by deaths and slaps.

## Slap and steal

Controls: keyboard F or visible SLAP button (mouse/touch). Empty hands are required. Attacks are rejected during tutorial, while knocked down, or in safe zones. The server chooses the nearest eligible live target within 8 studs and the forward cone (forward dot product >0.1), with an unobstructed raycast. Clients cannot nominate a victim or item.

Cooldown: 3 seconds, including misses. A successful hit knocks the victim down for 1.6 seconds, gives them 5 seconds of hit immunity, and transfers their one carried machine directly to the attacker if present. Hitting an empty-handed player still knocks them down. Server network ownership and an impulse implement the knockback; ownership returns to automatic on recovery. No damage or stored-inventory transfer occurs. Carry origin/time resets at transfer, preserving haul-time validation.

Safe zones: each yard's footprint (±40 X, ±43 Z around its center, all floor heights), plus 100 studs from shop arrival. Both attacker and target are checked. Respawns grant 10 seconds of hit immunity. Tutorial participants cannot attack or be hit. This deliberately protects the first delivery. Combat blocks teleports for 5 seconds. Pickup/deposit/drop are rejected while stunned.

## Compact yards and floors

Eight players / eight yards remain. Yard foundations shrink from 104×114 to 64×76 studs. Each ground floor has exactly eight display spots, in two columns of four. No unused ground-floor spots are prebuilt. Base upgrade terminal, future crusher pad and annex are removed.

Each Floors upgrade adds a physical level 16 studs higher, with eight pads, pillars, alternating side stairs and landings. Three purchasable floors give 32 total spots. The ExtraSlots pass grants one further complete eight-slot floor (improved from the previous five-slot benefit), maximum 40. Existing inventory is preserved during migration by rounding its old capacity up to whole floors. Inventory fills lower floors first. Replacing at capacity still requires explicit confirmation and cannot remove protected legacy items.

## Shop district and teleports

Shared plaza is north of the conveyor, centered near z=-229. Five colorful canopy stalls: Upgrades, Sell, Spin, Rebirth, Supplies. Shop arrival is (0,4,-196), leaving a short walk to each stand. Server interactions require a live player within 15 studs. Proximity prompts open their corresponding panels. Closing or walking away does not authorize a remote purchase from elsewhere.

SHOP → and HOME → on the right HUD teleport to the district / owned spawn. Five-second cooldown. Disabled while carrying, knocked down or in combat; carried junk must physically return home. They never sell or discard inventory. Currency, quests, collection and DROP remain accessible. Paid shop tabs and cosmetic spending are reached through Supplies, with server proximity checks for Core spending.

Sell stand lists stored, unprotected machines. The UI confirms permanent removal and displays the foregone income and exact Scrap payout. The server locates the UID in that player's profile, calculates SellValue from MachineConfig, and atomically removes the item and credits Scrap. Repeating a request cannot sell it twice. Protected legacy items are not sellable. No client-supplied price is accepted.

## Daily wheel

One free spin per UTC calendar day, reset at 00:00 UTC. Available immediately, including during tutorial, at the Spin stand. Prizes: 5 Cores (60%), 10 Cores (30%), 20 Cores (10%). Expected daily reward: 8 Cores. No cost, paid reroll, luck upgrade or Scrap prize. These Cores buy existing exact-price cosmetic styles.

Server rolls once per eligible request before the transaction. Reward and claimed-day marker share one Data:Commit. Save retries reuse the same reward. Repeat requests after success grant nothing. On ambiguous transaction failure, the session freezes and asks the player to rejoin; it cannot overwrite a possible successful reward. The wheel animation only follows the saved result, taking 2.5 seconds; closing it or disconnecting cannot remove an already saved reward. State retains the last prize. HUD indicates whether a daily spin is available.

## Rebirth

Rebirth stand offers an explicit review and permanent-reset confirmation. First requirement: 25,000 unspent Scrap; later requirements floor(25,000 × 2.5^current rebirth count). Maximum 10 rebirths. No carrying during reset. Each rebirth adds 5 percentage points to a permanent income multiplier, maximum +50%. Bonuses do not compound with themselves.

The atomic transaction resets ALL Scrap to zero, Income/Speed/Carry/Floors and legacy Slots/Expansion upgrades to zero, delivered-run count to zero, and removes all unprotected stored machines. Protected legacy inventory is preserved, with enough floors to hold it. No new starter is issued. Physical floors and movement speeds refresh after success.

Preserved: Cores, cosmetic ownership/equipped style, verified passes, collection discoveries, lifetime earnings, settings, receipts, daily quest progress/claims, daily spin claim/result and tutorial completion. This prevents repeating daily claims through rebirth. First-time fresh players have no protected items. Selling and rebirthing use profile transactions with fail-closed handling for ambiguous saves.

## Tutorial, HUD and balance

Tutorial sequence: grab a conveyor item → physically return it to the green intake → visit the Upgrade stand. No purchase is required to finish. Dropping or losing the first held item redirects the guide toward salvage. Stage persists. Migrated players with inventory skip the fresh tutorial. Bright outlined top objective, yellow world arrow and beam guide the next step. The guide reconnects to a respawned character and uses server-supplied positions with streaming.

HUD follows the supplied inspiration with light cards, dark outlines, colored action buttons and an outlined objective. Map ground and ambient lighting are brighter. Currency starts at zero; the first placed machine starts income. A mobile-friendly SLAP button supplements F. Existing responsive modal/scrolling behavior is retained. No screenshots of the rebuilt scene were rendered here; final positioning is a Studio acceptance check.

Income upgrades now add 6% of base income per level (previously 12%). Walking and carrying upgrades add 0.5 stud/second per level (previously 1), capped at 18 / 14. Floors start at 3,500 Scrap and grow ×3 per level. Full formula, costs and scenarios: ECONOMY_V3.md. Balancing assumptions require real playtest feedback, especially eight-player salvage availability and slap frequency.
