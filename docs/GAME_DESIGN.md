# V0.2 game design

Collect freely and frequently; slow major economic purchases. Fresh players start with one protected radio and four slots. Machines cost no Scrap to acquire. Duplicate deliveries count toward daily haul quests. A full yard can deliberately replace a non-protected machine at intake with no sale payout; this permits continued collecting without an infinite currency faucet.

Primary loop: see salvage → choose route → carry home → place/replace → earn passive Scrap → choose income, capacity, movement or annex → complete active contracts → earn Cores → choose a visible yard style.

No shop popup on joining. Right-side navigation exposes goals without covering the world. Upgrades can be inspected anywhere but purchased near the owner's terminal. Core styles and quest claims are available anywhere. All rewards, costs and ownership are server-authoritative.

Five progression branches are implemented. Security and fusion are deliberately not offered as functional upgrades because theft/fusion do not yet exist. Reserved crusher pads and restricted warehouse/gate signage communicate future content honestly.

Daily contracts reinforce deliveries, rare collection, upgrades and exploration. No playtime/AFK quest. A player can earn at most 40 Cores per UTC daily set. Purchases are deterministic cosmetics. Paid packs remain unconfigured.

Social opportunity comes from shared salvage and intersecting routes, visible carrying and yard displays. No theft implementation is implied. Travel and rarity races need two-client playtesting.
