# V0.2 map

Ground grows from 350×350 to 1000×880 studs (~7.2× area). Eight yard centers: X ±330, Z -210, -70, 70, 210. Main lots 104×114, annex 26×108. Gates face inward. Protected spawn near entrance, teal intake, amber terminal, 24 physical display pads, reserved crusher pad and expansion strip. Mechanical capacity begins at four; empty future positions visibly show room to grow.

Central plaza 120×210 surrounds the conveyor at X 0, with a hopper at its north end and magnetic gantry behind. Longitudinal roads at X ±242 join four cross roads. Direct diagonal concrete haul paths from yard gates to the conveyor reduce unnecessary right-angle detours. Freight stacks sit off those direct centerlines.

Spawn to nearest main salvage region is about 290–337 studs direct (~18.1–21.1 seconds at 16 studs/sec). Gate alignment and machine position add a few seconds; measure 18–25 seconds in Studio. Return carrying at 12/sec is roughly 24–30 seconds; maximum carry upgrade reaches 16. These are geometry estimates, not timed playtests. The first haul plus searching/placement targets 50–90 seconds. Corner-to-corner travel is longer.

Landmarks: 61-stud magnetic gantry, 81-stud water tower top, freight train and depot, twin warehouses, restricted-sector gate. Three glowing inspect stations support one daily exploration quest (not repeated farming). Restricted gate, warehouses and crusher pads are labeled future content and provide no pretend unlocks.

Effects: two steam vents, two distance-culled fans updated by one 20 Hz client handler, one looping client hoist tween, small label reveal on salvage arrival, eight worklights without shadows, restrained atmosphere/bloom/contrast and warm afternoon sun. Visual animations never control gameplay geometry or rewards. Sounds remain unconfigured rather than importing unlicensed audio.

StreamingEnabled stays on. Decorations are anchored; no per-prop Scripts. Tags reconnect local effects as instances stream. Floor boundaries and collision/path clearance still require Studio inspection. Benchmark a mid-range phone before asserting frame-rate targets.
